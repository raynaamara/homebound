Related tools and our focus

NASA/JPL Mars Trek supports Mars data exploration, terrain analysis and mission planning. ASU JMARS supports planetary-data analysis and observation-planning workflows.

HOMEBOUND complements these broader tools with a focused four-step workflow for one destination: compare observation-time choices, keep the complete round-trip budget visible, and save an explainable brief.

How these assumptions affect the model

The coefficients have no units. A value of 4 is not four minutes or a direct four-times multiplier. For each permitted step, the model uses:

grade = elevation rise / horizontal distance
time multiplier = 1 + uphill coefficient × max(grade, 0) + downhill coefficient × max(-grade, 0)

Illustrative coefficient examples
Step gradeCoefficientTime multiplier
10% uphill41.40 ×
10% downhill21.20 ×

The multiplier applies to the model's three-dimensional step distance divided by reference speed. A 10% grade is about 5.71 degrees, not 10 degrees.

A larger slope limit includes more terrain in the routing model. More reserve leaves less time for observation. These examples show how grade and your coefficients affect the travel-time estimate.

      Data, assumptions, and methods
      
    What each layer contributes
    • HiRISE optical: visual surface context.
    • HiRISE terrain: mean elevation and maximum native slope on the prepared model grid.
    • THEMIS: qualitative thermal-derived context at native 100 m spacing.
    • Mars Trek MGS/MOLA: a separate regional relief map locating the study area in Gale Crater; source spacing about 463 m, not used for routing.
    
      
    The grid covers about 1.52 × 1.52 km. Routing uses the prepared elevation/slope grid, selected terrain constraints, and diagonal-step rules.
    
      
    The starting preset uses 30 m/min, a 20-degree slope limit, and uphill 4/downhill 2 coefficients. You can inspect and edit these assumptions in Activities.
    
      
    Model scope: pre-departure terrain-based travel timing, observation duration, protected reserve, and plan comparison.
